# Brand: acme

Acme is a fictitious brand for the test suites. It is not bundled with the
brand skill; tests reach it through `brand.path` or by its folder path.

Files in this folder:

- `brand.yaml`: logo, caption ratios, colours and fonts.
- `logo.svg`: an amber disc (`#E69F00`), for pages.
- `logo.png`: the same disc, 200 x 200 px, transparent outside the disc, for
  the video caption.

## Video caption

The logo sits to the right of the creator caption. Its height is 1.3 times the
cap height of the caption; the ink gap is 0.45 times the caption font size.
