# Cool Graded Documentary Photo

## Style line

Documentary photograph with restrained cinematic colour grading: tinted shadows, natural skin tones, slightly desaturated, low contrast, quiet premium mood, soft even daylight from large windows, well lit, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field

## Default palette

cool navy and teal grade, teal-blue shadows

## Result

Person scenes and people at laptops; no text in the image. Labels and caption cards (`rgba(16,21,33,.88)`, 36 px) go on empty wall, sky or glass as HTML; screens are generated plain matte black and HTML screens composited with a perspective transform; logos pasted by script, never drawn by the model.

## Avoid

A sombre person on an opening slide (a viewer closes the film); a metaphor that does not read (a glass test room); a team of people around one laptop (read as staged diversity imagery); rows of identical laptops in an IT room (read as a laptop farm).

## Suits

Suits the person scenes of a documentary-style explainer film and the setting slides of a deck. Team, laptop-room and glass-room title scenes do not suit it (see Avoid).
