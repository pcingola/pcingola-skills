# Minimal Enterprise

## Style line

A clean, minimal enterprise infographic, flat modern vector style like a premium Linear or Stripe keynote slide. Pure white background, lots of white space, crisp thin lines. Sharp legible sans-serif text. No glow, no neon, no 3D, no drop shadows.

## Default palette

restrained palette: deep indigo, soft teal, one muted purple

## Result

Clean flat white infographic with the feel of a premium Linear or Stripe keynote. Works for almost any content: process flows, comparisons, capability lists, pillar summaries.

## Avoid

Neon, glow, dark background, Greek columns, 3D, photorealism, busy or cluttered layouts, heavy gradients, drop shadows, a large logo. Misspelled or invented words. A native rebuild done freehand from the text spec, guessing colours, positions and spacing: it reads as clip art, not a finished deck. Dense white documentation figures as video backgrounds: viewers read them as a slide deck.

## Suits

The house style of bespoke slide decks: the default choice unless the content calls for another style. Suits a series of decks on one subject, the explanation slides of a talk, a live demo deck and the scene images of a film explaining a process; it also works as image slides without native rebuild. Its one-central-diagram form does not suit a talk. In a film, card-and-line diagrams read as clip art, and dense documentation figures as a video background read as a slide deck.
