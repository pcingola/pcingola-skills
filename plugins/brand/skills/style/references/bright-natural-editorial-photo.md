# Bright Natural Editorial Photo

## Style line

Photorealistic high-end editorial photograph, full-frame camera, natural colour, bright and well exposed, soft natural daylight from large windows, gentle fill, no harsh shadows, the subject fills the frame

## Default palette

natural colour; white, light grey, warm oak, deep navy clothing, one warm accent

## Result

People at desks or a lab; screens either carry exact short labels and real-looking scientific images, or are generated black for a composited screen. A neutral even-daylight variant ("sharp throughout, accurate natural colour, unposed") reads as a real photograph.

## Avoid

Near-black images and a shrunk subject to keep a text area empty; abstract swirls or decorative networks on screens; a slide shown on a wall display ("a photo of a slide on a wall is still a slide"); gibberish or extra text, logos, tablets, cartoon molecules, extra fingers. The neutral variant can look like stock photography, and viewers may take it as footage of real people at a real site.

## Suits

Suits the summary and end scenes of an explainer film: photorealistic images work better there than page-drawn diagrams. The dark-boardroom, near-black and wall-display variants do not suit it (see Avoid). A main character who must look photogenic needs flattering soft light and a cinematic grade, which this style does not give.
