# Blue Hour Executive Editorial Photo

## Style line

Photorealistic high-end editorial photograph for a business magazine feature, full-frame camera, fast prime lens, shallow depth of field with smooth falloff, one strong directional key light from the side (a window or a practical lamp) shaping faces and objects with defined but soft-edged shadows, a cool fill so every face and hand reads clearly, cinematic colour grade, warm natural skin tones, rich contrast, not dark overall, real materials with visible texture: walnut grain, matte paper, wool and linen, brushed metal, glass

## Default palette

deep navy shadows, cool teal mid-tones, walnut, brass lamp, warm skin

## Result

Blue hour, warm lamp against cool window light; screens generated black for compositing; captions `rgba(16,21,33,.88)` with a teal rule.

## Avoid

Stock meeting and night-office scenes.

## Suits

Suits the problem scenes of a short film, with the darker images. Stock meeting and night-office scenes read as generic (see Avoid).
