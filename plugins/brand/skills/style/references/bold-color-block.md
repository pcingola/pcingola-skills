# Bold Color Block

## Style line

A clean bold poster-style enterprise infographic, flat modern vector style, solid flat color block panels tiling the canvas, high contrast white text and icons, no gradients, no texture. Sharp legible sans-serif text. No glow, no neon, no 3D, no drop shadows, no photorealism.

## Default palette

color block panels in deep indigo, soft teal and muted purple

## Result

Flat solid colour panels tiling the canvas with high-contrast white text: poster energy, the boldest of the three slide deck styles (with Minimal Enterprise and Editorial Big-Number). Suits a section divider, a three-part structure that dominates a deck, or a single slide that stands out from a run of Minimal Enterprise slides. Text has to stay short to survive the high contrast.

## Avoid

Dense or long content. Neon, glow, gradients, 3D, photorealism, busy or cluttered layouts, drop shadows, a large logo, muddy colours, low-contrast text.

## Suits

A style for a designed slide deck, used sparingly: it does not fade into the background of a deck the way Minimal Enterprise does.
