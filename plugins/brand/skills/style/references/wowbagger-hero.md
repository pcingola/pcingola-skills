# Wowbagger: Hero

The look of the main and hero images of the Wowbagger project.

## Style line

Painted sci-fi cartoon illustration, inked character with painterly backgrounds, a bored, scruffy, unshaven traveller with long messy hair and heavy-lidded eyes, in a worn hooded robe with a battered backpack, lounging on a rock and holding a mug with a short sarcastic slogan, above a vast alien desert at dusk with tall rock spires, a distant city on a cliff, a huge planet and a small moon low in the sky, warm sunset light against a deep evening sky, title in large hand-brushed lettering and a handwritten tagline with a brush underline
