# Engraved Print Diagram

## Style line

Engraved or etched monochrome print, fine line hatching, light line images on a dark ground, serif type, thin double frames, a faint grid

## Default palette

navy, cream and gold; a variant with a deep plum #6B2D5C slab

## Result

Diagram labels baked in.

## Avoid

Serif, engraved or monochrome treatments did not match a film of photos and matte diagrams.

## Suits

Suits a standalone diagram with a print look. It does not match an explainer film made of photos and matte diagrams.
