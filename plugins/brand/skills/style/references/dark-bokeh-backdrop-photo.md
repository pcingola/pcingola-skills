# Dark Bokeh Backdrop Photo

## Style line

Photorealistic photograph, dark and strongly out of focus (very shallow depth of field, everything soft bokeh), dark overall and low contrast, with soft even glow spread across the frame and no bright hot spot or hard high-contrast area in the centre, calm, moody

## Default palette

deep navy and charcoal tones with gentle blue-violet light

## Result

Mean luminance about 0.12. The scene page adds an overlay `rgba(14,20,38,0.58)` and an 8 px blur, with paper figures on white cards on top.

## Avoid

Bright white screens in the centre (a rejected take).

## Suits

Suits blurred backdrops behind overlaid cards in a film.
