# Editorial flat vector

## Style line

Style: bold editorial flat vector illustration, strong limited palette, characters with personality and slightly exaggerated proportions, crisp geometric shapes, no gradients, confident composition

## Default palette

palette of emerald green, coral, navy, mustard yellow and cool grey on a cream background

Magazine-style flat illustration. Distinct shapes and colours keep each person and screen readable when a camera zooms into part of the figure.
