# Hand Built Physical Model Photo

## Style line

Photograph of a hand-built tabletop physical model, museum-quality craftsmanship: oiled walnut, brushed brass, clear glass, cotton paper, natural linen thread, set on a matte dark slate surface with a dark slate-grey backdrop, softly lit so the background reads mid-dark grey, never black, large soft studio softbox light, gentle soft shadows, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, slightly elevated three-quarter view, no plastic, no glow, no screens

## Default palette

walnut brown, brass, warm paper white, threads dyed deep indigo and teal, one amber accent

## Result

Mechanisms as museum-quality objects; a diagram form builds nodes from cream paper cards on brass pins joined by waxed linen thread on a walnut board, labels typed on cream card or manila tags. Headlines can be baked in as white sans on the backdrop; page text is greeked as plain grey ruled lines.

## Avoid

Generated text on book spines and tags garbles; keep text to the few tags named in the prompt. A copperplate engraving look: fine line work turns grey at projection distance, and engraving invites period lettering the model garbles.

## Suits

Suits the title and section openers of a talk, the title and question slides of a live demo deck, and the key scenes and diagrams of an explainer film, where it marks the moments the film turns on.
