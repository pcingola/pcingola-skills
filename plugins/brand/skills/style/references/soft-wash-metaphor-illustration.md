# Soft Wash Metaphor Illustration

## Style line

Flat metaphor illustration on white, soft painterly gradient wash fading out at the edges, pale shapes with a light outline, no boxes or cards, labels set directly on the picture, flat, no shadows

## Default palette

soft teal #6AD0DC family wash, white and soft indigo shapes, indigo labels

## Result

An iceberg: white sky, thin waterline across the full width, small pale tip, wide pale body in tinted water. Generated with text, then a text-free plate kept as background raster with all text native, or text painted out and replaced by text boxes.

## Avoid

No record.

## Suits

Suits one metaphor image on a deck slide, with all text native. One image can serve several decks on the same subject.
