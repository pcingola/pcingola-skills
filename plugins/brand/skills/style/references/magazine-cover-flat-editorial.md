# Magazine Cover Flat Editorial

## Style line

Bold flat editorial illustration in the style of a technology magazine cover, limited palette, strong simple geometric shapes, slight print grain texture

## Default palette

limited palette of deep navy, teal, warm orange and off-white

## Result

No text in the image. HTML cards go in the empty third; a real application screenshot is composited on a flat screen.

## Avoid

A cheerful diverse crowd can read as staged diversity imagery.

## Suits

Suits a title with HTML cards in the empty third.
