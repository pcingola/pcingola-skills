# Large-Format Archive Photo

## Style line

Large-format architectural documentary photograph in the manner of Candida Hofer and Andreas Gursky, made with an 8x10 view camera: perfectly level, symmetrical one-point perspective, everything sharp from foreground to far wall, even natural daylight and quiet architectural light, no dramatic lighting, quiet, slightly desaturated colour, real materials with real wear and dust, fine-art photographic print quality

## Default palette

navy, teal, paper white, warm wood, a small amount of amber

## Result

Monumental archives and a safe-deposit vault. No text in the image; HTML captions with a brass rule `#c9a35a`.

## Avoid

A laptop lid facing the camera.

## Suits

Suits most scenes of a film about stored knowledge or records.
