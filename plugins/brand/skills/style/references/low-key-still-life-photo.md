# Low Key Still Life Photo

## Style line

Photorealistic still-life photograph, objects on a dark surface fading into darkness, one low warm key light raking across the scene, weak cool fill, real shadows, shallow depth of field, cinematic, nothing glows

## Default palette

near-black charcoal #0B0D14 to #151A2A with an indigo cast, dark wood or concrete, copper, off-white paper

## Result

A long dark lab bench with grouped instruments, a lightbox film and a brain model; or a blackened steel box with copper pipe, paper and twine on dark concrete. Text goes on as a flat print overlay, never on objects.

## Avoid

Faint unreadable marks on instrument panels (blurred in the final). Object still lifes of badges, pigeonholes or flat lays.

## Suits

Suits a dark frame of a film or a talk. Object still lifes do not suit a documentary-style film (see Avoid).
