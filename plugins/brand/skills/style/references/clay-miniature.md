# Clay miniature

## Style line

Style: soft 3D clay miniature world, plasticine figures and objects with rounded forms and fingerprint texture, tilt-shift photography with shallow depth of field, soft warm studio light

## Default palette

palette of sage, muted teal, terracotta, soft blue and cream pastels

## Result

Rounded plasticine people and furniture on a textured landscape, sharp in the middle band and blurred at top and bottom. Warm and friendly.

## Avoid

Small text over the textured ground: it lowers legibility. Footers and captions go on light bands.

## Suits

Suits a warm, friendly explainer film on a technical subject, for example a software process.
