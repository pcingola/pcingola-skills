# Scientific Feature Cutaway Illustration

## Style line

Editorial scientific illustration in the tradition of a Nature or Science feature article: a commissioned explanatory cutaway illustration of a mechanism, finely rendered with precise ink line work, matte materials, clean and exact like a technical illustrator, dark ground darkening to near black at the edges, faint paper grain, generous empty space, clear air, one soft studio key light from the upper left, soft contact shadows, nothing glows

## Default palette

deep ink indigo #1B1B4B ground, paper #F2EEE6, steel greys, teal #6AD0DC and purple #8064A2 accents

## Result

Headline and leader-line labels baked in, flat on the image.

## Avoid

No record.

Suits a labelled cutaway figure.
