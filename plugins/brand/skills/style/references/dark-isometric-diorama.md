# Dark Isometric Diorama

## Style line

STYLE: high-end isometric 3D miniature diorama render, 30-degree isometric camera, slight tilt-shift depth of field, deep gradient background, clearly coloured, not black, the scene well lit by a large soft overhead area light and a warm fill, so every object is clearly readable, matte and satin materials only: satin dark platform tops with brushed edges, warm white paper, matte card stock, cardboard, linen book cloth, powder-coated steel, at most a thin, subtle edge highlight along a platform rim (a fine line, no glow halo), one restrained accent on a few small objects only, restrained, editorial, calm, like a premium product visualization or architectural model photograph

## Default palette

navy #0d1830 to #142441 gradient, dark-slate platform tops, paper white #f2eee6, cyan-teal rim, amber #f4b353 accent

## Result

No text in the image; print shown as tiny grey lines.

## Avoid

Busy scenes with many small objects, saturated colours, text or labels inside the diorama.

## Suits

Suits the title scene of an explainer film. It is clean but less distinctive than other title styles.
