# Light Scientific Editorial Illustration

## Style line

Light, clean scientific editorial illustration, in the manner of a figure in a high-end neuroscience journal review, off-white background, fine linework with flat, softly shaded fills, structures in a pale neutral tone with the structure of interest in the accent colour, matte surfaces, even lighting, generous empty space for overlay text

## Default palette

background #F7F6F2, linework slate navy #1F2A44, structures pale warm grey #E4E1DA, structure of interest blue-violet #6C6FD1, second accent coral #E0675A, green #8CC152 only where facilitation is shown

## Result

No text in the image; sans-serif labels on white cards added by the scene page.

## Avoid

Scenes that are mostly white or empty background.

## Suits

Suits explanatory figures with overlay labels. A film needs images that fill the frame (see Avoid).
