# Bright High-Key Product Photo

Bright, crisp product-style documentary photograph with a cool colour grade, clean and confident, high key, well lit by bright morning daylight from a large window on the left, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field

## Default palette

navy and teal grade, pale light oak, white, a green plant
