# Wowbagger: Pale card

The look of the lighter persona images of the Wowbagger project, for example Reviewer 2 and the Nerd.

## Style line

Inked comic caricature illustration, clean confident outlines with light hatching and soft painterly colour, exaggerated faces, a smug, knowing character in the foreground doing something petty with complete seriousness among ordinary bystanders who are unaware or alarmed, a tidy, ordinary setting (conference hallway with posters, meeting room with a whiteboard) in soft, pale, hazy daylight with gentle shadows, props carrying short legible labels (a name badge, a sticky note, a whiteboard line) that deliver the joke
