# Technical blueprint

## Style line

Style: technical blueprint and cutaway engineering drawing, fine line work on dark paper with a faint grid, isometric cutaway views, hatching, dimension lines and callout circles without any numbers or text, no colour fills except subtle tints of the line colour

## Default palette

fine white and cyan line work on deep Prussian blue paper, subtle cyan tints

## Result

White and cyan line work on dark blue.

## Avoid

One model drew navy lines on a white grid instead of light lines on navy; that take was rejected.

## Suits

Suits a section divider on challenges or constraints, for example the divider that opens the problem section of a deck, and a repository logo.
