# Semi Realistic Digital Painting

## Style line

Semi-realistic digital painting, like high-end concept art or a book cover: realistic anatomy and lighting, painted rather than photographic, soft brushwork in the background, sharper detail on faces and hands, natural window light

## Default palette

muted navy, teal and warm amber palette

## Result

No text in the image.

## Avoid

The face may drift more between scenes than in simpler styles. It can look like stock art.

## Suits

Suits illustrated person scenes.
