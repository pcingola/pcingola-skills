# Vogon

The look of the images of the Vogon project (deadpan corporate satire about compliance software).

## Style line

Flat-shaded editorial cartoon illustration, clean dark outlines and smooth cel shading with subtle texture, a heavy, warty, toad-like alien bureaucrat with a jowly scowl and half-closed eyes, wearing a black suit, white shirt and black tie, stamping documents with a rubber stamp, set in front of a large solid disc on a plain paper page, office props with short legible labels (stacked binders labelled GxP, VALIDATION, COMPLIANCE, AUDIT, a mug with a slogan such as PER SOP or IT DEPENDS, a document stamped REJECTED), any human beside him a frazzled, panicking developer, the same rendering also used as a numbered comic strip with caption boxes and speech bubbles, no gradients, no glow, no 3D

## Default palette

cream paper #FAF8F3, ink #0A161E, disc red #752222, stamp red #BF100F, skin grey #929192, suit charcoal #3C3C43, paper shadow #D8CDC4
