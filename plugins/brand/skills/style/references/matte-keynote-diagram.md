# Matte Keynote Diagram

## Style line

Matte keynote diagram on a dark ground, a solid slab with columns standing on it, small coloured data images (ribbon protein, heatmap, survival curve) in the columns, thin connector lines, short sans-serif labels, flat matte surfaces, no glow, no glass

## Default palette

navy and deep plum #6B2D5C, coloured data images, gold #E8B12E for section labels

## Result

Diagram labels baked in; narration text set beside the shrunk diagram, never on top of it.

## Avoid

Serif, engraved or monochrome treatments do not match a film in this style; glow, neon and glass (see neon-glass-diagram).

## Suits

Suits the diagram scene of an explainer film made of photos and diagrams.
