# Light Room Editorial Photo

## Style line

A bright, airy, open room with white plaster walls, oak floor and furniture, stone surfaces, tall windows letting in abundant soft natural daylight on a clear day, a few large plants, directional window light from the side gives gentle, soft-edged shadows that model faces and objects, so the image has depth and is not flat, true photographic contrast and real blacks in small places, never washed out or hazy

## Default palette

warm whites, pale oak, light stone, fresh plant greens, natural skin tones, one small deep teal or navy accent

## Result

No text in the image; screens composited.

## Avoid

Smiles one notch too strong in the first takes.

## Suits

Suits the solution scenes of a short film, after darker problem scenes.
