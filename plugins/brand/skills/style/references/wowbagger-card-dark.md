# Wowbagger: Dark card

The look of most persona images of the Wowbagger project, for example the Mathematician, the Philosopher and the Bureaucrat.

## Style line

Inked comic caricature illustration, heavy confident outlines, dense cross-hatching and painterly colour, exaggerated faces, a smug, deadpan expert in the foreground doing something absurd with complete seriousness while one horrified or exhausted victim reacts in the background, a dim, cluttered, slightly grimy room (chalkboard, stacks of paper, old books, worn furniture, cracked plaster) lit by a bare bulb or low warm window light, deep shadows, props carrying short legible labels or a single line of chalk text that delivers the joke

## Default palette

amber light #D7AB6B and #B0824E, ochre #93653A, brown #6F543A and #5C422D, shadow #372C22, ink #1D130E, accent red #B8322A
