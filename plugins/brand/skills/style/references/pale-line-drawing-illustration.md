# Pale Line Drawing Illustration

## Style line

Thin, even line icons and line drawings, compose the slide around one strong central picture with generous empty space, very light cool off-white background, clean humanist sans-serif, pale soft fills, one warm accent used sparingly, no grid of boxes joined by arrows, no white cards with coloured top bars

## Default palette

deep indigo #1B1B4B, purple #8064A2, soft teal #6AD0DC, dark teal #1F8A99 for teal text, mid grey #5A5A66, warm gold #D39A2C as sparing accent, pale teal #E6F6F8 and pale lavender #EFEBF5 fills on #F7F8FA

## Result

An open book with a schema, a dotted route across a soft relief map of database cylinders, a layered stack of thin isometric plates with small person icons and hairlines running down through it; nearly white, calm. As a title-slide accent it sits at the right edge with the title beside it.

## Avoid

No record.

## Suits

Suits the light slides of a talk and a title-slide accent, kept as raster with native text.
