# Graded Reportage Photo

## Style line

Unposed documentary photograph, shot on a 35mm camera with available light, slight film grain, colour grade, reportage style, real wear, cluttered realistic details, natural skin texture, nobody looks at the camera, no text, letters, numbers, logos or signage, no glow, no holograms, no floating UI, no neon, no lens flare, no plastic skin, no stock-photo smile, no perfect symmetry

## Default palette

cool navy and teal grade

## Result

No text in the image. Logos pasted onto screens; model-drawn badge text painted out.

## Avoid

A person who looks depressed in a closing image.

## Suits

Suits the title, summary and closing photos of a documentary-style film.
