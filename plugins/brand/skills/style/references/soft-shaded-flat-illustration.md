# Soft Shaded Flat Illustration

## Style line

Editorial flat illustration with soft shading, thin dark outlines, simplified people, flat fills

## Default palette

muted navy, teal, warm peach and warm off-white

## Result

Outlined figures in a pastel room. A documentation-figure form bakes in labels.

## Avoid

The people read as drawings.

## Suits

Does not suit a documentary-style film: the people read as drawings.
