# Editorial Big-Number

## Style line

A clean editorial / magazine-style enterprise infographic, pure white background, generous asymmetric white space. Elegant sans-serif typography mixing a bold display weight for numerals with a lighter weight for body text. Flat, no 3D, no drop shadows, no neon. Flat vector illustration style.

## Default palette

restrained palette: deep indigo, soft teal, one muted purple used sparingly as accents

## Result

No cards, no boxes. Oversized pale numerals as step markers, magazine typography, hairline rules instead of borders. More confident, more typographic and less like a software interface than Minimal Enterprise. Suits sequences and ranked lists where the steps or points are the content: a numbered plan, a top-N list, a short narrative arc.

## Avoid

Content that is inherently grid-like (many parallel items, comparisons). Neon, glow, dark background, Greek columns, 3D, photorealism, busy or cluttered layouts, heavy gradients, drop shadows, a large logo, boxed cards.

## Suits

One of the three styles of bespoke slide decks, with Minimal Enterprise and Bold Color Block.
