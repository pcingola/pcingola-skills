# Dark Glowing Isometric 3D

## Style line

Isometric 3D render on a deep dark field, objects on dark platforms with glowing rim-lit edges, ghosted wireframe for items not yet built, thin light sans-serif type

## Default palette

deep navy, cyan glow, amber for open items

## Result

Slide text baked in; a film adds reveal masks and glows, no new words.

## Avoid

Across several scenes of a film it makes serious work look like a product launch.

## Suits

Suits a dark summary deck. It does not suit several scenes of one film, where serious work looks like a product launch (see Avoid).
