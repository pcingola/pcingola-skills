# Risograph Editorial Illustration

## Style line

Refined contemporary editorial illustration in the manner of a premium business magazine (New Yorker or Monocle style): no black outlines at all, every form built from soft colour shapes and light, visible risograph-like paper grain and subtle stippled texture, gentle shading with soft light, slightly more realistic proportions and a more mature, natural face than a cartoon

## Default palette

restrained muted palette of navy, teal, warm amber and warm off-white

## Result

No text in the image. With an outlined image as reference the model kept most of the outlines.

## Avoid

Black outlines, ink lines, line art, cartoon, flat vector. It may still read as drawing-like.

## Suits

Suits illustrated scenes of a short film.
