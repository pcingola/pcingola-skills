# Backlit Cut-Paper Diorama

## Style line

Photograph of a handmade backlit layered cut-paper diorama, built from many thin sheets of matte paper stacked in depth inside a shallow box, each layer cut by hand with a precise scalpel, lit from behind and from the side so each layer edge glows softly with warm paper light and casts a soft shadow on the layer behind it, translucent paper shows its fibres where the light passes through, shot with a medium-format camera, shallow depth of field, real paper texture, slight imperfections in the cuts

## Default palette

palette strictly limited: deep navy paper, teal paper, warm paper white, and one small amber accent

## Result

No text in the image.

## Avoid

A glowing open book sprouting luminous fibres (glow and magic-book imagery); faceless silhouettes; gothic gates and fantasy bridges unrelated to the scene.

## Suits

Suits calm, crafted scenes without text. Glow and magic-book imagery read as fantasy (see Avoid).
