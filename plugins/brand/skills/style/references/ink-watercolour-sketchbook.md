# Ink and watercolour sketchbook

## Style line

Style: hand-drawn travel sketchbook, loose confident black ink lines, transparent watercolour washes that bleed past the lines, lots of untouched white paper, washes fading out softly at the edges of the page

## Default palette

palette of soft indigo, warm ochre, sage green, rose madder and a few touches of red

## Result

Airy, light and elegant; low contrast. Worked with a bird's-eye open-plan composition.

## Avoid

Zooming in or dimming the image: with the low contrast, detail fades.

## Suits

Suits airy bird's-eye scenes without zoom.
