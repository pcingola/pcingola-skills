# Neon Glass Diagram

## Style line

Dark diagram of neon-edged glass panels and glowing tiles, bloom, braided glowing flow lines, saturated colour, glossy reflective floor

## Default palette

navy with saturated cyan, magenta, violet and gold glow

## Result

Labels baked in on panels and tiles. An isometric form puts glass towers on a dark slab with neon-lit edges and glowing cables to outcome plinths.

## Avoid

Generic glowing icons in circles (DNA, molecule, database, human outline). Neon glow, glass, bloom and saturated rainbow colour read as cheap AI imagery.

## Suits

Does not suit a diagram scene that needs a restrained look: it reads as cheap AI imagery (see Avoid).
